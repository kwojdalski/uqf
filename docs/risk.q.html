risk.q

Entity Details

.qrisk.carry_pnl

Money value of that same forward premium/discount on a given notional.

eg: .qrisk.carry_pnl[1000000;1.10;1.1050] -> 5000f
Return: the carry P&L, in quote currency
Parameters:
  • notional - position size in base currency units
  • fwd - outright forward rate
  • spot - spot rate

.qrisk.carry_return

Forward premium/discount implied by spot vs. an outright, as a decimal return: (F-S)/S. Positive means the base currency trades forward at a premium (i.e. rf is below rd under CIRP).

eg: .qrisk.carry_return[1.10;1.1050] -> 0.004545455
Return: the decimal forward premium/discount
Parameters:
  • fwd - outright forward rate
  • spot - spot rate

.qrisk.pip_value

Money value of a 1-pip move on a given base-currency notional, in quote currency.

eg: .qrisk.pip_value[1000000;10000] -> 100f
Return: the value of a 1-pip move, in quote currency
Parameters:
  • notional - position size in base currency units
  • pip_factor - 10000 for most pairs, 100 for JPY crosses

.qrisk.pnl

Mark-to-market P&L of a spot position, in quote currency.

eg: .qrisk.pnl[1000000;1.1000;1.1050;1] -> 5000f
Return: the P&L, in quote currency
Parameters:
  • notional - position size in base currency units
  • side - 1 for long base currency, -1 for short
  • entry_rate - the rate the position was opened at
  • exit_rate - the current/closing rate

.qrisk.var_historical

Historical simulation VaR: the magnitude of the (1-confidence) empirical percentile of a series of historical P&L outcomes.

eg: .qrisk.var_historical[-100+til 200;0.95] -> 90 (5th percentile of a 200-outcome series)
Return: a positive loss estimate, in the same units as pnl_series
Parameters:
  • pnl_series - a list of historical P&L outcomes
  • confidence - one-tailed confidence level, e.g. 0.95 or 0.99

.qrisk.var_parametric

One-sided parametric (variance-covariance) VaR for a position with annualised volatility vol over a horizon of t years, at a one-tailed confidence level (e.g. 0.95, 0.99).

eg: .qrisk.var_parametric[1000000;0.10;1%252;0.95] -> 10361.6 (1-day 95% VaR)
Return: a positive loss estimate, in the notional's currency
Parameters:
  • notional - position size (sign ignored - VaR is a loss magnitude)
  • vol - annualised volatility, decimal (0.10 = 10%)
  • t - horizon, year fraction (e.g. 1%252 for one trading day)
  • confidence - one-tailed confidence level, e.g. 0.95 or 0.99
