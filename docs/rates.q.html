Entity Details

.uqf.cont_to_simple

Convert a continuously compounded rate to the equivalent simple rate over the same period t.

eg: .uqf.cont_to_simple[0.04879016;1] -> 0.05
Return: the simple rate with the same growth over t
Parameters:
  • r - continuously compounded decimal annual rate
  • t - year fraction

.uqf.df_cont

Continuous-compounding discount factor.

eg: .uqf.df_cont[0.05;1] -> 0.9512294
Return: exp(-r*t)
Parameters:
  • r - decimal annual rate
  • t - year fraction

.uqf.df_simple

Simple-compounding discount factor.

eg: .uqf.df_simple[0.05;1] -> 0.952381
Return: 1/(1+r*t)
Parameters:
  • r - decimal annual rate
  • t - year fraction

.uqf.growth_cont

Continuous-compounding growth factor.

eg: .uqf.growth_cont[0.05;1] -> 1.051271
Return: exp(r*t)
Parameters:
  • r - decimal annual rate
  • t - year fraction

.uqf.growth_simple

Simple-compounding growth factor.

eg: .uqf.growth_simple[0.05;1] -> 1.05
Return: 1+r*t
Parameters:
  • r - decimal annual rate
  • t - year fraction

.uqf.simple_to_cont

Convert a simple-compounded rate to the equivalent continuously compounded rate over the same period t.

eg: .uqf.simple_to_cont[0.05;1] -> 0.04879016
Return: the continuously compounded rate with the same growth over t
Parameters:
  • r - simple decimal annual rate
  • t - year fraction
